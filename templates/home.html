What you can do

Two Powerful Modes

Switch between the Mixer and Emphasizer to explore every aspect of Fourier analysis.

⚡
FT Magnitude/Phase Mixer
Load up to 4 images, adjust component weights with sliders, choose inner or outer frequency regions, and watch the IFFT result update in real-time.
PART A
◈
Region Selection
Draw a resizable rectangle on the frequency domain. Choose inner (low) or outer (high) frequencies. The selected region is highlighted with a blue overlay.
PART A
⊞
Smart Resize Policies
Unify image sizes to smallest, largest, or a custom W×H. Toggle aspect ratio preservation. All four images are synchronized automatically.
PART A
◐
FT Properties Emphasizer
Apply 10 operations: shift, complex exponential, stretch, mirror, make even/odd, rotate, differentiate, integrate, windowing, and N-fold Fourier.
PART B
⬡
Duality Illustration
Apply any action in the spatial domain and see its FT counterpart — or flip it and apply directly in the frequency domain. Duality at its finest.
PART B
◉
Brightness / Contrast Drag
Drag on any viewport to adjust brightness (up/down) and contrast (left/right) independently, per panel, in real-time — including FT components.

Workflow

Three steps to insight

01
Load Images
Double-click any viewport to load a grayscale image. Up to 4 images for mixing, 1 for the Emphasizer. Drag to adjust brightness and contrast instantly.
02
Configure & Mix
Choose between Magnitude/Phase or Real/Imaginary mixing modes. Adjust per-image weights, select a frequency region, and hit MIX — or let auto-mix do it live.
03
Explore Properties
Switch to the Emphasizer, pick an action from the dropdown, tweak parameters, and watch both spatial and frequency domain viewports update in real-time.
